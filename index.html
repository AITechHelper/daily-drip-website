Our Story

A father, a daughter,
and a calling.

Daily Dr!p started with Fern and her father — a pastor who has spent his life in ministry, and a daughter who works alongside him. Coffee became their next act of service: something to build together, rooted in the same faith that has always shaped their family.

Every bag carries that purpose forward. Fern and her dad started this business to help families walking through a cancer diagnosis — because a cup of coffee, shared with intention, can be part of someone's healing.
